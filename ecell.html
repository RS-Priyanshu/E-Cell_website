What We Do??

The E-Cell at IIMT University, Meerut, is a vibrant student-led initiative.
It fosters entrepreneurial spirit, providing resources and mentorship for budding entrepreneurs.
Through events and collaborations, it transforms innovative ideas into businesses.
Join us at E-Cell, where ideas meet execution.

ORGANIZING ENENTS

The E-Cell hosts various events,
providing a platform for students to
showcase their entrepreneurial skills and
learn from industry experts.

CONDUCTING SEMINARS

The E-Cell arranges seminars with
experienced entrepreneurs and business leaders
who share their insights, inspiring
students to venture into entrepreneurship.


PROVIDING MONTERSHIP

The E-Cell offers mentorship to
students with startup ideas, guiding
them through the process of transforming
their ideas into viable businesses.

GROUP DISCUSION

The E-Cell organizes group discussions
that allow students to brainstorm and
refine their business ideas in a
collaborative environment.


ON CAMPUS SESSIONS

The E-Cell conducts on-campus
sessions to elevate the skills of students,
preparing them for the challenges
of entrepreneurship.
